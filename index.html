About

About

I study computer science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, with a 3.9 GPA and a 2029 graduation date. This past summer I worked at the Qatar Computing Research Institute on software that tells real photos apart from AI-generated ones. Before that I helped build a tool that flags fake news articles.

In my free time I build projects that fix things students around me actually complain about. IlliniHousing puts every off-campus apartment listing in Champaign-Urbana in one searchable place, and CampusPulse puts every club event in a single feed. I've also spent a semester as a course assistant, sitting with 300+ students while they learned Java.

Most of my work comes down to the same three things: cleaning up messy data, testing hard enough to find where something quietly breaks, and wrapping it in something a person can actually use. Mostly Python for the machine learning side, TypeScript and Next.js on the web, Java for Android.
